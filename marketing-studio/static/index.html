How bulk works

Each row is rendered with your active brand and saved to outputs/bulk_… (also downloadable as one .zip). Rows that fail are skipped and listed so the rest still finish.

Ideas: a product list → one ad per product · a week of offers → a week of stories · the same script in 3 themes to A/B test · testimonials → quote cards.
